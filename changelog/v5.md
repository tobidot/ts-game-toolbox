# 5.x

[All major versions](README.md)

5.0 dropped the MVC part and the "Abstract" module to keep the toolbox to general helpers. From 5.4 on, each minor version brings the modules of a wave of the [Spielwerk series](https://www.game-object.de/pages/spielwerk), one per step.

## 5.6.0 (2026-11-02): Spielwerk steps 11 to 14

- `physics/aabb`: `overlaps`, `resolve`, `sweep`, `move`, `union` (step 11).
- `camera/Camera2D`: dead zone, bounds, look-ahead, parallax (step 12).
- `world/TileMap`: a layered tile map over `Grid2D`, `from_ascii` (step 13).
- `canvas/PixelCanvas`: a pixel buffer, Bresenham lines, flood fill, snapshots (step 14).

## 5.5.0 (2026-11-02): Spielwerk steps 6 to 10

- `physics/kinematics`: `integrate`, `projectile_position`, `flight_time`, `range`, `apex`, `launch_velocity` (step 06).
- `input/ComboMatcher` and `ComboList` (step 07).
- `input/PointerInput`: mouse, touch and pen as one (step 08).
- `data/HsvColor` and `RgbColor.to_hsv` (step 09).
- `flow/spawner/Spawner` and `weighted_pick` (step 10).

## 5.4.0 (2026-11-01): Spielwerk steps 2 to 5

- `input/KeyboardState`: key state per frame (step 02).
- `data/Grid2D`: a generic grid with lines, neighbours and `map` (step 03).
- `flow/state/StateMachine`: enter, exit and update phases (step 04).
- `flow/timers/Timer` and `Cooldown` (step 05).

## 5.3.1 (2026-11-01)

- Unused parameters removed in `TestConsoleLogElement`.

## 5.3.0 (2026-11-01)

- `dom/VideoSplash`: `play_video_splash` plays one video over a container and resolves with `ended`, `skipped`, `timeout`, `blocked`, `error` or `aborted`, and cleans up after itself.
- `Image` and `Video` widgets: the vendor-prefixed smoothing flags are set through a typed record.

## 5.2.0 (2026-11-01)

- Theme and event refactor, the baseline for the Spielwerk series.
- `CheckBox` no longer takes a label argument.
- Vitest 5.

## 5.0.x and 5.1.x (2025-09 to 2026-05)

- 5.0: the MVC part and the "Abstract" module are removed.
- 5.1.x: maintenance releases; their notes are only in the git history.
